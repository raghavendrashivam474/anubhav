# 🧠 Anubhav

> Preserve experiences today. Retrieve wisdom tomorrow.

Anubhav is a Personal Wisdom Preservation System designed to help people capture life lessons, observations, experiences, and insights before they are forgotten.

Unlike traditional note-taking applications, Anubhav focuses on transforming experiences into actionable wisdom that can be surfaced when relevant in the future.

---

## Why Anubhav?

People forget lessons.

They repeat mistakes.

Valuable experiences fade with time.

Anubhav helps users:

* Capture experiences
* Extract lessons
* Preserve wisdom
* Retrieve insights when needed

---
